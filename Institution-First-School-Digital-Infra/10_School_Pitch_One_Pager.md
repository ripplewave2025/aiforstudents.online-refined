# One-page pitch: Put the School Online (Institution-first)

## What this program is
A 90-day, teacher-centred rollout that makes the school digitally functional as an institution:
- official identity, accounts, and shared repository
- teacher workflows that reduce workload
- responsible AI use for staff
- measurable proof for replication

## What the school gets
- domain + official email system (or cleaned governance of existing)
- shared drive repository and templates
- teacher PD pathway (Level 1–4)
- community-based adoption system
- metrics dashboard and pilot report packet

## What the school provides
- approval from school head
- 1 admin steward
- 2 teacher champions
- 45 minutes weekly review time
- (optional) 1 shared laptop or desktop access

## Why now
Schools can have phones around them and still be institutionally offline.
This program builds the institution backbone so teaching and management become faster, safer, and more continuous.

## Safeguards
- teacher-first
- no student-facing AI in this phase
- no personal data into public AI
- human verification of all outputs

## FAQs (short)
Q: Will this replace teachers?
A: No. The goal is to reduce non-teaching workload and support teaching quality.

Q: Phones are not allowed in school.
A: This is institution-first; workflows can run on staff devices and shared school devices.

Q: What happens if staff changes?
A: Ownership is institutional: admin stewardship, shared drives, documented handover.
