# Teacher Community and Discovery System

## Goal
Make adoption self-sustaining through a small, safe community of practice.

## Community format (minimum viable)
- One group channel (Google Group / Teams / WhatsApp for announcements only)
- One shared resource folder (institution-owned)

## Weekly ritual (non-negotiable)
Every week each teacher champion posts:
1) One workflow used (with screenshot or link)
2) One friction (what blocked it)
3) One improvement suggestion

## Monthly ritual
- 30-minute “show & share” session:
  - 1 success story
  - 1 template shared
  - 1 challenge solved together

## Roles
- Teacher Champion A: facilitation
- Teacher Champion B: resource curation
- Admin Steward: account and access support
- Program Lead: coaching + quality + governance

## Community safety rules
- No student personal data posted in the group
- No screenshots showing private info
- Treat drafts as drafts; verify before official use
