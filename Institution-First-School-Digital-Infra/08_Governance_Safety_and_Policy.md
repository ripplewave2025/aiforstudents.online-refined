# Governance, Safety, Data Protection, Responsible AI (Staff)

## Purpose
Prevent breakdown from staff changes, and prevent privacy/AI misuse.

## Governance model (minimum)
- 2 admin accounts (primary + backup)
- shared password manager or sealed recovery record (principal-owned)
- role-based access (admin vs teacher vs viewer)
- institutional ownership of domain and repository

## Account lifecycle
Onboarding:
- create official email
- add to staff group
- add to shared drive with correct role
- enroll in Level 1 PD

Offboarding:
- transfer file ownership
- remove group access
- archive email (if required)
- revoke access tokens

## Data protection principles
- collect minimal data needed
- keep personal data out of public AI tools
- store institutional data in approved repositories
- report breaches quickly

## Staff Responsible AI policy (template)
Allowed:
- drafting lesson materials (verify)
- rewriting/simplifying/translating (verify)
- summarizing documents and minutes
- creating templates and checklists

Not allowed:
- entering student personal identifiers into AI tools
- automated judgments about students or staff
- generating official disciplinary decisions without human review
- sharing confidential documents in public AI systems

Verification rule:
“AI can draft. Humans decide.”

## Incident response quick plan
- detect → contain → assess → notify principal/admin → remediate → document
