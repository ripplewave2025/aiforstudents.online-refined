# Mission, Vision, Principles

## Mission
To enable safe, equitable, and teacher-led adoption of digital infrastructure and AI-supported staff workflows in schools, beginning with institutional identity and teacher capacity.

## Vision
A future where no school is institutionally offline in the digital era, and where teachers have the tools, training, and community to teach better, manage less, and sustain improvement.

## Core idea (institution-first)
A school can have phones in the community and still be offline as an institution.
We build the school’s digital backbone first: identity + governance + continuity + teacher workflows.

## Principles
1) Teacher first, always.
2) Institution continuity over individual convenience.
3) Equity by design: low-bandwidth, offline-ready, inclusive.
4) Responsible AI: human verification, no sensitive data leakage, bounded use.
5) Evidence over hype: adoption and time-saved must be measurable.
6) Interoperability and portability: avoid lock-in and single-person ownership.
7) Community sustainment: sharing practices weekly is part of the system.

## Success definition (pilot phase)
- The school has a functioning domain + official accounts + shared repository.
- Two teacher champions actively use workflows weekly.
- Teachers report measurable time saved.
- The school can onboard a new teacher without losing documents, templates, or access.
