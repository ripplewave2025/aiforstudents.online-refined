# NEP 2020 Alignment

## Purpose
This document makes the rollout defensible in NEP 2020 language and priorities.

## Alignment map (short)
A) Teachers at the centre → teacher-first workflows + PD + community
B) Technology for teaching + planning/management → institution-first digital backbone
C) Equity and digital divide → low-bandwidth workflows, shared devices, offline options
D) Pilot-first implementation → 30/60/90 day pilot with metrics

## Quotations to anchor (to be maintained)
Maintain a short “quote bank” (8–12 lines) with:
- teacher centrality
- technology integration
- digital divide cautions
- online platforms for teacher tools and monitoring
- public digital infrastructure and standards
- CPD expectations and online modules

## How we interpret NEP in this project
1) Digital is not an add-on; it supports planning, management, and continuity.
2) Teacher effectiveness is the primary outcome; student-facing comes later.
3) Adoption must be equitable; design for the lowest-resourced context.
4) Implementation must be iterative and evidence-based.
