# Metrics, Proof, and Reporting

## Why metrics
Without proof, this becomes a “tool training” project, not institutional transformation.

## Metrics categories
A) Institutional readiness
- Domain set up (Y/N)
- Admin accounts (Y/N)
- Staff accounts activated (%)
- Shared drive live (Y/N)

B) Adoption (weekly)
- Weekly Active Teachers (count)
- Documents created in shared repo
- Forms used (count)
- Meetings logged (count)

C) Workload offload (monthly)
- Self-reported hours saved per teacher (range)
- Number of reused templates/assets

D) Safety and governance
- Policy acknowledgements (%)
- Incidents (count) and resolution time

E) Community health
- weekly posts
- shared assets added
- peer problem solved count

## Reporting formats
Weekly (1 page):
- wins
- blockers
- key metrics
- next actions

Monthly (2 pages):
- adoption trend
- time saved estimate
- artifacts created
- risks and mitigations

## Proof packet (end of 90 days)
- baseline vs endline snapshot
- 20 reusable templates
- 10 meeting minutes with closed actions
- sample assessment workflow
- governance checklist + admin handover doc
