# Institution-First, Teacher-Centred AI & Digital Infrastructure Rollout

## What this repository is
This repository is the operating documentation for an institution-first rollout that:
- puts schools online as institutions (identity, administration, continuity, planning),
- supports teachers as the first users,
- enables responsible, bounded AI usage for staff,
- starts with a pilot (Lamahatta) and produces replicable proof.

## Scope boundary (current)
IN SCOPE:
- school identity: domain, website, official email and admin ownership
- staff accounts and workflows
- teacher professional development (PD) and community building
- responsible AI usage for staff (drafting, planning, summarizing, translation)
- metrics and reporting for adoption and time saved

OUT OF SCOPE (for now):
- student accounts and student AI usage
- student data collection beyond minimal operational needs
- any surveillance, monitoring, or automation that affects student rights
