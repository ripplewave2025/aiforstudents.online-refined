SCHOOL DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE PROPOSAL

aiforstudents.online · 2026


**THE VISION and Core GOALS**
1. Offload the repetitive tasks of teachers to AI and Give teachers resources and the time to think deeply and improve the quality of education they want to provide to their students.
2. provide students access to AI tools for studies, tracking their progress visually, testing their capacity, creativity and critical thinking with full teachers oversight.
3. Equip Principals and Staffs with digital resources and global connections to upgrade infrastructure, secure grants, Scholarships, foster competition, innovation and discovery among schools Worldwide - all at their fingertips.
4. Finding a way to deliver World-class digital infrastructure for full AI readiness in Every rural schools.
5. Our goal is to equip teachers with the tools to go where the students are - their smartphones.

No underserved school should be left behind in the age of AI.

**WHAT YOUR SCHOOL ALREADY HAS — AND THAT IS ENOUGH**

At least one working internet connection
At least 2 computers or smartphones (or smartphone is enough)

Nothing else is needed to get started.

**THE PROBLEM**
Today’s students live on screens, easily accessing AI tools for their studies while teachers are left without oversight. The tools to fix this are completely free for schools, but they are trapped behind a technical 'domain wall.' Setting up this foundational IT infrastructure is the fastest way to bridge the gap between elite and rural schools, finally giving underserved educators the visibility they need.

A school domain works exactly like an Aadhaar card. Without Aadhaar, you cannot access government schemes, bank accounts, or benefits — even though they exist and are meant for you. Without a domain, the tools exist, they are free, and they are meant for your school — but you cannot access any of them.

One domain. One decision. Everything activates.

**WHAT WE ARE ACTUALLY DOING**
We are not installing software. We are not buying hardware.
We are opening the door to the age of AI in education — for teachers and students who deserve it just as much as any school in Delhi or Kolkata. Within one year, what teachers and students do with these tools will speak for itself.
This is the first step. Like registering for Aadhaar before everything else became possible.

**WHAT GETS UNLOCKED — FREE, FOREVER**

Google Workspace for Education — Gmail, Classroom, Drive, Meet. Unlimited accounts for every teacher and student. Teacher sees every student's progress live.
Microsoft 365 Education — Word, Teams, OneNote, Excel. Free for every teacher.
Canva for Education — Posters, worksheets, presentations. Free for every teacher.
AI Learning Tools — AI tutors in Hindi and local languages, with diagrams. Every student gets a private tutor at home.
Student visibility — See who submitted, who didn't, who is falling behind. In real time.
Official school identity — Every teacher gets name@[schoolname].in permanently.
Many other possibilities.
This is just the beginning.

**THE 30-DAY PROCESS**

Headmaster signs one authorization letter + provides school registration certificate. (10 minutes)
Domain registered. Google Workspace for Education applied. Zero cost to school.
One 2-hour training session. Teachers onboarded. First assignment posted on Classroom.
One teacher becomes permanent admin. School owns everything. Independent forever.


**WHAT THE SCHOOL NEEDS TO DO**

Provide school registration certificate + authorization letter on school letterhead
Nominate one teacher as internal point of contact
Attend one 2-hour training session

That is all. Everything else is handled.
